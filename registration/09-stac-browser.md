The outcome of the harvesting is best visualised via [STAC Browser](https://github.com/radiantearth/stac-browser), already deployed as part of the Data Access BB.

### **Open STAC Browser**

Open the [STAC Browser Web UI]({{TRAFFIC_HOST1_89}}/browser/).

> Harvesting can take some time. Refresh the view in STAC Browser to see ongoing registration.

Navigate through the [Planetary Computer harvest]({{TRAFFIC_HOST1_89}}/browser/#/external/eoapi.eoepca.local/stac/collections/sentinel-2-l2a) collection. If you completed the optional Landsat and/or Sentinel steps, their harvested items are also visible: [Landsat]({{TRAFFIC_HOST1_89}}/browser/#/external/eoapi.eoepca.local/stac/collections/landsat-ot-c2-l2), [Sentinel]({{TRAFFIC_HOST1_89}}/browser/#/external/eoapi.eoepca.local/stac/collections/sentinel-2-c1-l2a).

> The Sentinel jpeg2000 assets cannot be visualised by STAC Browser.
